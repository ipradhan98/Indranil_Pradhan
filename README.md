## 🧑‍💻 About me

Electrical engineer completing my **M.Eng. in Electrical & Microsystems Engineering** at OTH Regensburg (2026). I have ~3 years of hands-on industry experience at **Schaeffler** and **Vitesco Technologies** covering:

- ⚡ Power electronics development — OBC, DAB, LLC, NPC, eAxle inverters
- 🔬 Model-based development — MATLAB/Simulink, PLECS, Stateflow, System Composer
- 🖥️ PCB design — 4/6-layer mixed-signal boards, STM32, FPGA, PMIC, gate drivers
- 🧪 Hardware validation — CAN, CANoe, INCA, HIL testing, oscilloscopes
- 📊 Simulation & loss/thermal modeling for SiC, GaN, IGBT semiconductors

> 🎓 Master's thesis @ Schaeffler: 11 kW single-stage OBC — improved converter efficiency by ~2%
